About Fyyna

Your Trusted Partner in
Custom Metal Manufacturing

Shanghai Fyyna Machinery Tech Ltd. is a premier metal products company based in Shanghai, China. With ISO 9001:2015 certification and over 15 years of hands-on experience, we specialize in producing high-quality custom metal parts for customers in the US and China.

We provide resources and expertise in custom metal parts that reduce your direct and overhead costs. Our dedicated Manufacturing Operation can transform your unique concept into a finished part — manufacturing to your specifications, drawings, or world standards.
